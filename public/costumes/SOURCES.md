# Costume artwork

These unmodified game sprites were retrieved on 29 October 2026 from
[PokeMiners/pogo_assets](https://github.com/PokeMiners/pogo_assets), under
`Images/Pokemon - 256x256/Addressable Assets/`. Filenames retain the costume
and form identifiers. `.s.icon.png` is the shiny counterpart.

All artwork belongs to its respective Pokémon GO rights holders. PokeMiners'
repository describes its asset collection as educational material; these
images are not original artwork created for this guide.

The associated event, shiny-release and costume-evolution references are
listed per family in `src/data/costumes.js`. Sprite existence alone is not
used as evidence of release or an allowed evolution.
